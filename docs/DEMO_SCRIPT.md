# Sponsor demo (about five minutes)

Open the app, choose **Open the Metro Roofing demo**, then use **Demo Mode** (top right) or **Demo Mode → Start presentation guide** for on-screen presenter notes. Everything runs live: real records, real software calculations, real OpenAI tool calls. Metro Roofing and all names are fictional.

**Before presenting:** header shows **AI live**. Click **Demo Mode → Reset demo** so findings, drafts and tasks start empty. A full scan takes about 25–40 seconds.

## Scene 1 — Meet the business
- Say: Metro Roofing tracks estimates in Excel and invoices in QuickBooks. The owner wants more sales without hiring an administrator, but follow-ups and collections slip.
- Show the Overview: open pipeline, overdue balance, capabilities ready, all calculated from their records. "No AI investigation has run yet."

## Scene 2 — Activate intelligence
- Click **Run AI Intelligence Scan**. No question is typed.
- Narrate the console: the Intelligence Engine orients itself, then dispatches the Readiness, Sales and Financial agents in parallel with specific assignments. Blue lines are AI decisions; grey lines are software tools running on the records.

## Scene 3 — Discover opportunities
- Point to **Priority intelligence** and the briefing. Open the **Intelligence Inbox**, click the top finding: explanation, software-computed value (labeled "potential pipeline value" or "outstanding balance"), supporting records with source file and row.
- Optional: **Ask about this** → the Advisor inspects the finding's records and explains the prioritization.

## Scene 4 — AI prepares work
- Open the **Action Center**: personalized follow-up and payment-reminder drafts written from the records, plus tasks, each linked to its finding. Open a draft, edit a line, **Approve**. It says "approved, not sent". No email is connected.

## Scene 5 — Owner takes action
- **Demo Mode → Apply a sample change → Record payment** (pays the largest overdue invoice) or **Contact top estimate**.
- Show the toast: software immediately re-verified findings (resolved / updated). Financial Intelligence shows the new overdue total.

## Scene 6 — Intelligence adapts
- **Demo Mode → Run follow-up scan**. The console first shows software re-verification, then agents re-check and update findings instead of repeating resolved issues. Resolved findings appear under Inbox → Resolved and in the Action Center's outcomes as "verified outcome".

## Scene 7 — Sponsor impact
- **Sponsor Dashboard** → sign in as the fictional Northstar program. Show adoption, readiness, and the **AI work and outcomes** strip: AI-prepared actions, owner-approved actions and software-verified outcomes are counted separately. No business names or records are visible.

## Alternate: readiness walkthrough
From the landing page, **Start Metro Roofing from scratch** runs the original onboarding → upload → "missing contact dates" → fix story. Each import now automatically starts an intelligence scan of the affected area.
